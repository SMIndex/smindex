How to read this

A large short is a place to look, not evidence. Brent fell, so everyone short it is profitable — that is the definition of the position, not skill or foreknowledge. What is worth reading is concentration (a position built in minutes rather than days), pure takers who crossed the spread every time instead of quoting, and entries clustered near the high. Rows flagged MM are market makers and are mostly noise.

Size covered is the honest caveat on every row: where it reads well under 100%, the wallet built most of its position outside our fill record. Position history does not exist for HIP-3 markets at all, so we can date a wallet's first visible opening fill but not what it held before that.

What we still cannot see

Entry times come from fills. Position history does not exist for this market.

The position sweep calls clearinghouseState without a dex parameter, so HIP-3 markets are invisible to it and analytics_positions holds no Brent rows. We can date a wallet's first visible short-opening fill, but not what it held before that, nor whether it quietly flipped from long. Fill coverage also starts at the date in the header — anything built earlier is invisible, which is why several rows show a position far larger than the fills backing it.
